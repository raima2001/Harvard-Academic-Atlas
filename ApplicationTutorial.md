# 🚀 How to Register/Sign Up

### **Step 1: Access the Registration Page**

![image_2](https://github.com/aditya-saxena-7/ac215_masalachai/blob/milestone4/vault/screenshots/2.png)

1. 🌐 Open your web browser.
2. 🔗 Navigate to the Academic Atlas registration page by entering the following URL: [http://104.198.147.52:5000/](http://104.198.147.52:5000/).

### **Step 2: Sign Up**

Please keep the following guidelines in mind when creating your account:

1. 📧 **Your email address must belong to one of the following domains:**
   - `g.harvard.edu`
   - `fas.harvard.edu`

2. 🔒 **Password Criteria:**
   - **Length:** Must be at least 8 characters long.
   - **Special Character:** Must include at least one special character (e.g., @, #, $).
   - **Numerical Value:** Must contain at least one number.

### What Happens Next?

Upon completing the registration process, two key events will occur automatically:

1. **Redirection to Sign In/Login Page:**
   - 🔄 You will be directed to the Sign In/Login page, signaling that your registration was successful.
2. **Welcome Email:**
   - 📧 Look out for a **welcome email** from **harvardacademicatlas@gmail.com** in your registered email inbox.
   - 📨 The subject line will read: **Welcome to the Harvard Academia Atlas Powered by MasalaChai 🚀**.
   - 🔍 Check both your **Inbox and Spam/Junk folder** to ensure you receive this important email.

# 🤖 Section 2: Academic Bot Builder

### **Step 1: Navigating to the Academic Bot Builder**

![image_4](https://github.com/aditya-saxena-7/ac215_masalachai/blob/milestone4/vault/screenshots/4.png)

- 🔗 **Access:** Visit [Academic Bot Builder](http://104.198.147.52:5000/persona_dashboard) to start customizing your own academic chatbot, designed to assist with course recommendations, scheduling, and resource guidance.

### **Step 2: Creating Your Academic Bot**

1. **Naming:** On the Persona Dashboard, name your bot and provide a brief description (under 80 characters).  
   Example: **"Course Guide Bot - Helping you navigate your academic journey!"**

### **Step 3: Defining Bot Behavior**

- 🛠 **Instructions:** Craft a detailed prompt explaining how your bot should operate, behave, or respond.  
  Example: **"I am a bot specialized in guiding students with course recommendations, helping them create schedules, and providing academic resource suggestions for the courses held at Law School."**

### **Step 4: Categorizing Your Bot**

- 🏷 **Selection:** Choose one or more categories that best fit your bot’s purpose (e.g., Academic, Business).

### **Step 5: Assigning a Bot Image (Optional)**

- 🎨 **Customization:** Add an image to your bot to enhance its identity and relevance.

### **Step 6: Enhancing Bot Capabilities**

- **Features to Enable:**
  1. 📂 **Retrieval:** Links uploaded documents from the Student Resource Vault to the bot. Attach up to 20 items for tasks requiring detailed knowledge or answering questions based on extensive data.

- 🗂 **Note:** Resources uploaded to the Student Resource Vault (see Section 1) can be directly linked to your bot for improved functionality.

### **Step 7: Finalizing Your Bot**

- ✅ **Activation:** Enable the desired features and click **"Add Academic Bot"** to complete the creation process.  
- 🎉 Your bot is now ready and listed in your Academic Bot Library!
- Click on the **Go To Chat** and start your counselling!

# 📊 Section 4: Usage History

### **Step 1: Accessing Usage History**

![image_7](https://github.com/aditya-saxena-7/ac215_masalachai/blob/milestone4/vault/screenshots/7.png)

- 📍 **Location:** The **[Usage History](http://104.198.147.52:5000/usage_history)** feature can be accessed from the dropdown menu in the top-right corner of the page.
- 🖱️ Click on **Usage** from the dropdown to view your activity details.

### **Step 2: Understanding Usage History**

The **Usage History** page provides a detailed record of all your interactions with Academic Bots and the Student Resource Vault. It tracks every activity to help you monitor and manage your usage effectively.

### **Features of the Usage History Page**

1. **Activity Tracking:**
   - 🕒 **Timestamp:** The exact date and time of each interaction.
   - 📌 **Title:** The title or name of the Academic Bot or resource accessed.

2. **Token Consumption:**
   - 🔢 **Input Tokens:** Tracks the number of tokens consumed for each interaction, helping you monitor usage costs and optimize efficiency.

### **Step 3: Why Use the Usage History Page?**

- 📊 **Monitor Token Consumption:** Stay informed about the tokens consumed during each activity to manage your usage effectively.
- 📝 **Track Interactions:** Keep a live record of all activities for better accountability and reporting.
- 📈 **Optimize Performance:** Analyze how your bots and resources are being used to improve their functionality.

### **Pro Tip:**
🔔 Use the **Usage History** page regularly to ensure your token consumption aligns with your academic and project goals. This feature helps you stay informed and manage resources efficiently.
